# Assets Inbox 📥

Drop any new images, logos, videos, or files here and I'll organize, optimize,
and wire them into the site. Don't worry about formats or sizes — I'll handle that.

## What's especially useful
- **Photos of the school** — classrooms, play areas, outdoor space, the building/entrance
- **Photos of activities & events** — kids learning/playing (with permission to use)
- **Teacher / staff photos** (optional, for an About page)
- **Logo files** — any higher-res or vector (SVG/PDF/AI) versions of the Siam Kid D logo
- **More mascot / doodle art** in the same hand-drawn style
- **Brand stuff** — exact brand colors, fonts, or a style guide if you have one

## Optional info (a plain .txt or note is fine)
- School email address to list on the Contact page
- Opening hours
- Age ranges you accept
- Anything you want on the new About page (history, milestones, awards)

## Tips (totally optional)
- Group things in subfolders if you like (e.g. `classroom/`, `events/`, `logo/`)
- Phone photos straight from your camera are perfectly fine — I'll resize/compress them
- For Facebook/YouTube videos, just paste the links in a note instead of uploading

I'll also reuse the good assets from your old repo (the egg-bird mascots, doodles,
logo, and event videos), so you only need to add what's *new*.
