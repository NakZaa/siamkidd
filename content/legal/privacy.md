# Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Siam Kid D School ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with the [Siam Kid D School](https://siamkiddschool.com) website (the "Site"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling personal data in accordance with Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act, B.E. 2562 (2019) ("PDPA").

## Information We Collect

This Site is an informational website. It has no contact forms, accounts, or sign-ups, and we do not run advertising or analytics trackers. As a result, we do not actively collect personal information about you through the Site.

If you choose to reach out to us by phone or Facebook Messenger, any information you provide (such as your name and message) is used solely to respond to your enquiry.

## Cookies

The Site itself does not set cookies and does not use analytics or tracking technologies. Embedded third-party content (see below) may set its own cookies or collect data when it loads.

## Third-Party Services and Embedded Content

Some pages include content provided by third parties. When that content loads, the third party may receive your IP address and device information and may set its own cookies, governed by their own privacy policies:

- **Google Maps.** The Contact page displays an embedded map from Google Maps, which loads when you view the page.
- **YouTube.** Video sections show a thumbnail by default and only load the YouTube player when you click play. The player is loaded in privacy-enhanced mode (`youtube-nocookie.com`). Thumbnails are served by Google.
- **Facebook and Messenger.** Links to our Facebook page and Messenger open services operated by Meta.

We do not control these third parties. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of Google and Meta.

## How We Use Information

We use any information you provide to us to:

- Respond to your questions and enquiries.
- Operate and improve the Site and our services.

## How We Protect Your Information

We take reasonable measures to protect any information you share with us against unauthorised access, alteration, or disclosure.

## Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. We may disclose information only when we believe in good faith that it is required to:

- Comply with applicable law or a lawful request.
- Protect against, identify, or address unlawful acts.
- Protect the rights, property, or safety of any person, in accordance with the law.

## Children's Privacy

This Site is intended for parents, guardians, and other adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children through the Site. Information about enrolled children is handled directly with their parents or guardians as part of our school services, not through this Site.

## Your Rights

Subject to the PDPA and applicable law, you may request to access, correct, update, or delete personal data we hold about you, or to withdraw consent. To make a request, please contact us via our [Contact page](/contact).

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

If we make any material changes to this Policy, they will be reflected on this page. We encourage you to review it periodically.

## Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Policy, please contact us via our [Contact page](/contact).
